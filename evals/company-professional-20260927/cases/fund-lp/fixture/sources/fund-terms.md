# F1 agreement excerpts for this synthetic transaction

The F1 bank ledger records CNY 120 million received in connection with the proposed exit, after the transaction's already-settled costs. **For pro forma waterfall arithmetic only**, assume this CNY 120 million is the amount to allocate once the necessary sale and distribution approvals and closing conditions have been verified. The bank receipt alone does not establish legal distribution readiness. There are no additional fund-level taxes, reserves, offsets or expenses against this CNY 120 million in this synthetic arithmetic. The hypothetical waterfall applies in precisely this order:

1. Return CNY 100 million contributed capital to LPs.
2. Pay CNY 8 million **cumulative preference** to LPs.
3. The remaining profit is split: 20% performance allocation (carry) to GP; the other 80% to LPs. No GP catch-up, clawback, hurdle reset or other waterfall clause applies to this transaction.

The LP share of this transaction (capital, preference and residual after GP carry) is allocated 40% to LP A and 60% to other LPs, matching their contributed-capital ratio. Manager M's annual management fees are separately billed and **not deducted** from this CNY 120 million transaction. The general fee is 2% of commitment for the full 2026 year; signed LP A side letter changes LP A's rate to 1% for that same year, with no other discounts, credits or proration. All due dates, distributions and notices still require the applicable real approval and execution evidence.

An affiliated sale requires a separate LPAC approval under this fictional F1 agreement, with conflicted interests disclosed and handled independently. Investment committee (IC) approval cannot replace LPAC approval. This source is a synthetic agreement excerpt, not a determination of applicable public law.
